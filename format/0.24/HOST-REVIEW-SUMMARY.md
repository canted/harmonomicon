# Candidate 0.24 host review summary

The new author instruction is “invite two people; accept the first complete valid typed contribution; advance the saved queue.” It composes with existing collection/grouping and qualified starting material. Meaningful selection and deadline behavior is package-defined; hosts provide clocks, serial transactions, actual media authority, invitations and successor launch.

The losing invitee goes to the front with a fresh shared window after the winner's contribution. The previous canonical author stays excluded. Expiry moves both back against unchanged material. Decline replaces one ticket within the existing deadline and preserves the partner's ticket. Full failed passes and an internal churn cap stop with configurable cooldown; clock exhaustion cannot reopen.

Review [relay-notes](relay-notes.md) for exact transitions/events/output, [host packet](HOST-REVIEW.md) for executable acceptance criteria and [validation evidence](../../validation/0.24/EVIDENCE.md) for checks. Exact historical semantics remain retained. No activity-name dispatch, calendar delivery or unlimited workflow engine is included. Source queue consumption must be globally qualified, unique and committed with successor creation/grants/tokens. Read authority is independent of membership; every retained material and queue source must cover new viewers, including null-canonical queues and later configuration after restart. The demo supports preauthorized join/rejoin and denies new source-unbound readers.

This is an isolated local candidate for independent review. It is not merged or published, and no 1.0 decision is implied.
