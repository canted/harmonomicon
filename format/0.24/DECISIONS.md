# Candidate 0.24 decisions

1. **Accept a completed contribution atomically.** `first_valid@1` validates eligibility, ticket, exact canonical predecessor and actual ready owner-controlled typed value before committing canonical output/closure/queue. Claiming then composing and pool append followed by host close express different behavior.
2. **One bounded participant policy.** `policy:rolling_pair@1` shuffles once, invites two, puts winner back and loser at front, and rotates expired pairs. Keep independent per-invitee ticket identity when a partner is replaced. Do not expand to three or infer queue rules from activity names.
3. **Shared deadlines reset after each canonical success.** The authorized behavior deliberately renews the carry-over person's opportunity. It can keep an inactive person in one slot indefinitely while others contribute; it does not promise participation fairness or guaranteed turns.
4. **Explicit queue state crosses instances.** Contribution input carries material; `queueInputs` carries only the qualified saved queue policy state and canonical ref. Trusted all-viewer source attestation, exact ref matching and single consumption prevent forks or fabricated order. Historical contribution and operation versions retain semantics.
5. **Keep ongoing work in the host.** One invocation accepts one contribution or completes a failed pass/pause. Retry delay, exclusive deadline, prior-author exclusion and decline/membership behavior are language-defined; delivery and successor launching are host work. No unlimited workflow programming, calendar or notification engine.
6. **Membership and failure are bounded.** Retain surviving order, append authorized newcomers; withdrawals preserve historical identity and failed opportunities. Prefer unattempted people, including odd tails with one previously attempted partner. End after full pass or 100 failure transitions with distinct reasons. Non-resumable clock exhaustion never truncates a duration.
7. **Authority persists with actual origin.** Canonical text/image/audio is attributed and visible to bound viewers. Expansion rechecks every retained contribution and immutable queue for the entire expanded audience, through roster changes and later window configuration, including restoration and null canonical. The demo permits preauthorized people to join/rejoin and denies new source-unbound viewers. Queue membership is not a material/byte grant. Moderation, new cross-provider read grants and video remain outside scope.
8. **Validate reuse with a different composition.** Pair reflection after a first-valid creative piece uses retained grouping/privacy operations without activity-specific code. Its 24-hour windows, fixed even roster, empty-result instructions and pair-only note audience match the executable definition. Schema, conformance, two independent runtimes/SQLite hosts and exact retained comparison are candidate evidence, not full production integration.

No faithful historical migration count is added merely for a newly changed activity. The 0.8 competitive-handoff contract is precedent, not a contract silently rewritten here. Six complete 0.12 migrations remain six; fourteen remain incomplete. Independent review is separately coordinated at the implementation pin.
