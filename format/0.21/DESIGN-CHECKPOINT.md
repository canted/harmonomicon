## Bounded choices

The finite runbook is the initial continuation model (existing 32-step/64-ID bounds); linked outcomes stay within one instance. Voting can consume `artifact_pool@1` or `artifact_pool@2`, supporting text/image/audio. Candidate publication at voting entry is explicit voting behavior even for an originally private pool; it does not reveal unrelated pool entries. Effective candidate authors are frozen at source closure. Vote totals count current ballots of actors in the final effective voting set; withdrawn ballots remain in durable history/ledger. Candidate snapshots never change after voting entry.

Designated-person tie decisions with timeout/random fallback are deferred: they add authority and waiting states beyond the required simple tie policy. Result-derived eligibility is deferred: hosts may configure effective actors explicitly but cannot claim the package enforces exclusion of a previous winner. These are named limitations for faithful prospective host app support.

Video, more than one typed contribution per actor, balanced/two-offer assignments, self fallback, unassigned responses, unlimited streams, product ending/restart, SMS rules, late-after-settlement acceptance, calendars and general roles/workflow programming remain excluded. Existing text `pool@1` quotas remain supported; this candidate does not expand typed quotas.
