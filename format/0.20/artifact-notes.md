# Typed contributions and the host boundary

Candidate 0.20 adds four top-level operations and two capabilities to 0.19. Earlier directories and operation contracts are retained. It is an incremental pre-1 candidate; it neither defines nor declares 1.0.

## Exact contribution and distribution rules

`artifact_pool@1` has exactly `id`, `op`, `prompt`, `kinds`, `visibility`. `kinds` is a nonempty unique subset of `text`, `image`, `audio`; visibility is `private` or `group`. Each effective actor can submit one immutable entry; `itemId` is a nonempty scalar string unique within that pool. The exact payload is `{itemId, value}`. Text values are exactly `{kind:"text", text:<nonempty scalar string>}`. Media values are exactly `{kind:"image"|"audio", ref:<nonempty scalar string, <=256 code points>}`. No bytes, URLs or client authority metadata are accepted in place of a trusted reference.

`assign_artifacts@1` has exactly `id`, `op`, `source`, `policy`, `recipients`, `cardinality`, `reuse`, `unmatched`. Source must name a preceding typed pool. On closing that pool, freeze its final effective actor ordering; only accepted sources whose authors remain effective are eligible. Accepted entries withdrawn from eligibility stay in history and their original ledger. `contributors` selects effective authors with accepted sources; `effective` selects the whole frozen effective list. Self is always excluded. `cardinality:"one"`, `reuse:"allowed"`, `unmatched:"skip"` are the only supported choices. Empty/self-only pools yield unmatched recipients; no self fallback or wait-for-more recovery is inferred.

`policy:next_nonself_source@1` chooses the next eligible source author cyclically in frozen effective order. `policy:seeded_nonself_source@1` samples eligible accepted entries in acceptance order using the [retained exact seeded sampler](distribution-notes.md); recipients are processed in frozen effective order. Zero candidates consume no draw and one candidate consumes no draw. Outcomes and seed state persist; recovery/retry does not reroll. No cryptographic randomness or exposure fairness is promised. This is the same named selection algorithm with explicitly different typed-pool inputs, not a change to `assign_sources@1`.

`artifact_response@1` has exactly `id`, `op`, `source`, `prompt`, `kinds`. Source names a preceding typed assignment. A currently effective matched actor independently submits one exact `{itemId,value}` referencing their saved assigned ID. Allowed kinds follow the same value/authority rules. Acceptance saves the source identity/value/attribution alongside the response. No global choosing/composing barrier or participant-choice stage exists. A phase with no assignments skips immediately; otherwise it closes by effective date or trusted close, even when everyone has responded.

`reveal_artifact_responses@1` has exactly `id`, `op`, `source` naming a preceding typed response. It publishes accepted linked pairs with source and response attribution to bound viewers. It does not publish unused sources. All four operations are top-level only. They can precede/follow other supported top-level operations; no general concurrency, media routing/rating or nested typed collection is supplied. Semantic validation enforces references, capabilities and nesting in addition to structural JSON Schema.

## Trusted host inputs and controls

All new operations require `host_controls@1`; image/audio kinds also require the corresponding media capability. Opting into host controls permits a declared minimum of one participant and an organizer identity that also participates. Organizer identity gives no private-content bypass or universal application role. Older packages without the capability retain their distinct-organizer/minimum-two contract. Retained organizer-gated actions/projections still use the explicitly bound organizer (including the complete partition map). An overlapping identity participates wherever the original roster selects it; it gains no access to other actors' private contributions. Existing operations keep their initial participant roster; effective actor controls apply only to the new pool/response phases.

At instance creation the trusted embedding may supply `hostInputs`, a map from new pool/response step ID to exactly `{actors,opensAt,closesAt}`. Actors are unique non-system scalar IDs, at most 100 per phase; an empty list is allowed. Dates are null or nonnegative safe integer Unix milliseconds; two supplied dates must satisfy `closesAt > opensAt`. Null opening means phase entry time; null closing means no automatic deadline. Omitted phases use the initial participant list and null dates. Initial inputs are saved immutably; explicitly different inputs on restore reject. Initial bindings include the original roster, organizer and all supplied effective actors, up to 256 identities.

Only a trusted `system` event may `configure` or `close` the currently active new phase. Configure payload is the full exact control object; a nonnull closing must be later than the trusted current clock. Configuration can add eligible actors, withdraw actors, pause with a future opening, extend/shorten dates or remove the automatic deadline. New actors join bound identities (the 256 bound applies); host authentication/token issuance is separate. It cannot replace contributions, alter saved assignments, edit another phase or reopen a phase already settled at its deadline. `close` has an empty payload and may end a phase early, including before its configured opening. Effective ordering at source closure decides assignment eligibility. Later response eligibility edits do not reroll that assignment.

Opening is inclusive; closing is exclusive (`now >= closesAt` settles before a new action). Multi-day dates have no 24-hour phase cap. Dates must leave safe-integer room for the package's retained relative-duration budget; this conservative budget check is not a new duration limit on old operations. Clock, event ordering, exact accepted-event-ID replay and changed-reuse rejection follow the retained engine contract. An accepted control retry replays without reapplying it. Previously accepted contributions and read projections remain stable after eligibility changes; application authentication revocation is a host decision. Counts are visible even when content is private.

## Media authority, visibility and retries

Before a new media acceptance, an instance-bound trusted authority must attest submitting ownership, declared image/audio kind, readiness, immutable identity and retained accessible content. Runtime validates exact shape, reference length, declared kind, actor/source eligibility, quota, opening/deadline and ledger. Inaccessible, unready, foreign and wrong-kind references reject. The engine's `trustedMedia` registry is a test harness input, never an HTTP participant field. Accepted replay does not reauthorize or substitute a reference when a resolver later fails.

Private pool entries are visible only to their authors; group pool entries are immediately attributed to all bound viewers. Saved assignments expose only the recipient's source ID/value, without source attribution before linked reveal. Private response records expose only their author's accepted response; linked reveal exposes accepted pairs. Hosts grant byte reads from authenticated typed projections. Uploader access to their own ready unsubmitted bytes is separate from activity publication. Seeing a reference never grants submission ownership. Assignment read grants survive response eligibility withdrawal; identity revocation remains external.

Upload/processing and contribution acceptance are separate. On upload success followed by failed/uncertain submission, preserve the ready receipt and retry the intended event identity; a rejected event has no accepted ledger entry, whereas accepted changed reuse rejects. No partial form/contribution acceptance is allowed. Drafts, codecs/transcoding, byte authentication, durable blob retention and publication records belong to the host.

Discovery advertises local upload profiles: the existing bounded PNG profile and canonical PCM WAV (44-byte RIFF/WAVE header, one fmt/data chunk, PCM16 mono/stereo, 8–48kHz, nonempty aligned samples, <=524,288 bytes, exact sizes). These are validation-host choices, not language encoding restrictions. Opaque host-authorized audio references work without requiring WAV. Actual WAV validation checks raw chunk signatures; 32 valid/adversarial fixtures agree across hosts. PNG's 29 regression fixtures and corrected bounded decode/order/name checks remain unchanged.

## Three witnesses and honest boundaries

- [Simplified Chorus](examples/simplified-chorus.json): private image sources, one saved random non-self assignment, independently accepted text/audio response and explicit reveal. Host supplies effective actors and resolved multi-day dates; organizer may participate. This omits two-cover choice, balancing, late-third-source recovery, replacements/moderation and full product parity.
- [Feedback Round](examples/feedback-round.json): immediately public text/image/audio primary pieces by host-selected actors. Reviewer/follower labels and open replies live in the host. The durable test inserts a host-owned reply after primary closing while activity state stays unchanged; it does not claim a production discussion integration or permission-gated dialogue. If a later operation needs discussion output, a new explicit input contract is needed.
- [Daily music journal](examples/daily-music-journal.json): solo text/audio entry, participating owner, private projection and host-resolved instance date. The host can start separate daily instances with local-calendar dates, including 23/25-hour days. Privacy, audio and backfill choices are illustrative, not universal journal defaults. No calendar, recurrence scheduler, edits or automatic backfill policy is added.

[Engine checks](artifact_check.py) declare outcomes/privacy facts, compare independent engines/schema and restart at every boundary. [Durable checks](../../validation/0.20/artifact_trial.py) use authenticated uploads/grants, every-event recovery, source-ID and response races, close/submission races, partial retry, long deadline workers, unsupported/no-state checks and typed-exchange-to-private-pairs package transfer. This demonstrates composition without activity-name dispatch or Downfold changes.
