# Candidate 0.22 decision record

October 1, 2026. Base `5677de6`; a bounded next candidate following 0.21, with no merge/publication or 1.0 authorization.

1. **Existing reuse is real.** `pool@1` plus `for_items@1` already carries each accepted text slip into claim/read/reveal and later instructions in that body. `assign_sources@1` plus `respond@1` already creates linked responses to saved sources. Neither ordinary reuse nor output flowing to a later instruction was absent. The narrower new need is one selected typed contribution, preserving its real author/origin and authorized media, as the starting material of a separately launched instance.
2. **Declare material, not retrieval machinery.** An author names a required typed input and consumes it through `{binding:name}`. The host resolves an actual accepted contribution or selected output, attests the complete snapshot, checks every viewer and retains origin media. No lookup expression, activity-name dispatch, copied title or executable template belongs in the package.
3. **Material origin and handoff are different.** A globally unique UUID URN plus source step/item identifies the immutable material; author and originating round remain attached. A bounded `via` pointer identifies the contribution or decision that handed it onward. Retention may forward material from an older instance through a new decision without rewriting its origin.
4. **Version the coherent typed chain.** Qualified candidate references and typed result-source restrictions require new operation versions. Extending old vote/presentation versions to accept new sources or shapes would silently widen their historical contracts. The new versions retain familiar collect, vote, count, select, present and explicit reveal instructions; they do not introduce a new workflow language or wrap arbitrary old sequences.
5. **Fallback is a policy, not a composed repair procedure.** `select@2.noVotes` is omitted/unresolved by default, or deliberately chooses random eligible material or retains the usable starting source. Structured `basis` distinguishes votes from fallback. Counts are unchanged and the retained source receives no fabricated vote. Equal chances and durable one-time selection are specified without prescribing a generator.
6. **Absence of candidates is still absence of continuation.** Retention is limited to nonempty candidate pools without a counted vote. An empty pool stays `no_candidates`, even with a starting piece. This distinguishes lack of proposed continuation from lack of a decision and avoids a universal fallback. The retention example and walkthrough state this consequence explicitly. A future explicit empty policy needs its own justified contract.
7. **Visibility includes every actual viewer.** Input authorization covers the organizer, original roster, future-phase actors and later additions, including origin/author/round metadata. Missing/false/exceptional authority fails closed. Actor expansion rechecks every binding before any state or read-grant change. Viewing media never grants submission ownership.
8. **Hosts launch successors.** The five-step creative package ends after presentation. A host may start another instance with a declared binding to the real selection. Dates, calendars, actor management, media/provider trust, deletion and notification infrastructure stay host-owned. No automatic stream lifecycle is inferred.
9. **Consistency review favors fewer concepts.** Iterating all accepted text items, selecting an outcome and supplying an authoritative starting input are different author instructions. A wholesale reorganization would obscure those differences or change historical restrictions. The new contract defines qualified material once and uses that same shape in bindings, candidates, counts, outcomes and presentations; visibility, retry, eligibility and closing rules reference retained contracts rather than inventing parallel concepts. `via` is a bounded handoff relation, not another material model. Shared host-control/value helpers remain reusable; old operation versions are not rewritten merely to reduce the token count.

## Bounded limits retained

Designated-person tie decisions with timeout/fallback and result-derived submitter exclusion remain deferred: they need an authority/pending-result or eligibility contract. Multiple typed contributions (the authorized next-candidate direction), video, offer/exposure policies, unassigned responses, general calendars/roles/templates, unlimited streams and thread-ending/restart rules remain outside this profile. Cross-provider transfer infrastructure and production/distributed failure engineering remain host responsibilities. None is required by the creative package or held-out supplied-brief planning composition. Six completed historical migrations remain six.

The design checkpoint and independent read-only design review settled full-snapshot/viewer authority, immutable origin with separate handoff provenance and the exact outcome matrix before implementation. Independent Python/JavaScript execution and durable host evidence establish a bounded local supported profile, not complete product parity or production certification.
