# Jamuary

## Identity and context

Jamuary is a January challenge to **record and share a music jam each day**. The [Jamuary community introduction](https://jamuary.org/t/welcome-to-jamuary/7) describes musicians broadly; it does not require synthesizers. People make music separately and encounter one another through daily forum threads and links to recordings elsewhere.

## Documented procedure

1. For each January day, make and record a jam. A community organizer may post a prompt for that day; the prompt supplies inspiration rather than a documented mandatory constraint.
2. Share a link in that day's Jamuary forum thread, or publish elsewhere and link it. Other participants can listen and comment.
3. Repeat across the month. Sharing the final collection or playlist can extend the activity, but the introduction does not make a final album or prize a requirement.

## Rules and gaps

- The daily pace is the central challenge. The consulted community introduction does not define strict submission cutoffs, a penalty for missing a day, media-format validation, or a winner.
- A participant in the introduction described posting to Instagram first, then linking from the forum. That shows cross-platform activity, not a requirement for every participant.
- The source also includes a participant stepping away after finding the pace too heavy. That is evidence of a participation limit, not a general dropout rate.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** calendar recurrence, optional per-day prompt, creation outside the host, link submission, day-indexed gallery, listening/commenting, optional cross-platform distribution. A mediating app could publish prompts, accept audio or links, and surface responses. It need not produce the music or verify that each recording was made on that day unless the activity explicitly requires that.
