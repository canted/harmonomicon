# The January Challenge by 64 Million Artists

## Identity and context

The documented 2026 edition offered 31 different quick creative prompts. Individuals and organizers could use them with groups, teams, schools, libraries, and communities. The organization's site describes 2026 as its **last organized January Challenge**, so this card records that edition rather than a promised recurring service.

## Documented procedure

1. Participants receive or access the day's creative prompt. An organizer can download the complete 31-prompt pack ahead of time to plan group sessions.
2. Participants do or reflect on the prompt in their own way. They may share the creation, a reflection on the experience, or neither.
3. A group facilitator can arrange a live session, a daily prompt channel, breakout discussions, or later recognition of contributions. These are options in the organizer guide, not universal requirements of every participant.
4. Repeat for the January calendar; no ranked winner is described.

## Rules and gaps

- Prompt content changes daily, unlike a fixed self-chosen action repeated for 100 days. The 2026 prompts were designed by different people and groups.
- The organizer guide explicitly permits participation at one's own pace and multiple contribution modes. It does not require displaying every person's finished work.
- Source material does not prescribe a platform-wide completion gate or penalty for missed days.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** scheduled prompt series, organizer preview, local group facilitation, optional live co-creation, optional private/public reflection, cross-channel posting, recognition. The [online-hosting guide](https://64millionartists.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Hosting-The-January-Challenge-and-other-creative-activities-online.pdf) explicitly treats Slack, WhatsApp, social media, and video calls as places where the surrounding interaction may happen. An app could bring some of that mediation together, but the source does not define one required all-in-one flow.
