# 750 Words One Month Challenge

## Identity and context

People write privately each day while the platform makes some **completion status** public through monthly lists and badges. The shared object is accountability, not journal text. This is a group-related journaling challenge even though members do not co-author a journal.

## Documented procedure

1. Sign up for a named calendar-month challenge.
2. Write at least 750 words on each day of that month. The day is calculated from midnight to midnight in the participant's configured time zone; a previous day cannot be filled later.
3. If every day is completed, the participant appears on the success wall. A missed day places their name on the failure wall, but they may continue writing and may try the next month.

## Rules and gaps

- The challenge does not judge literary quality; it counts words. The [site description](https://site.750words.com/) calls the writing private and describes points and streaks.
- The challenge page uses the labels “Wall of Awesomeness” and “Wall of Shame.” Those are observed product terms, not proposed protocol language.
- The consulted pages do not show group comments on the private writing or a shared prompt. They also do not specify how to handle a disputed time-zone setting in an ongoing challenge.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** recurring local-day window, measurable completion, private content/public status split, missed-day state, month-long aggregate result, opt-in public accountability. A portable host could track a completion claim or verified word count while keeping the text private. The source's no-backfill rule means a simple editable streak counter would change this activity.
