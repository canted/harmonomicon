# Focusmate paired coworking

## Identity and context

Focusmate coordinates two people who work on their **own separate tasks** during a scheduled live video session. It is included as a parallel-practice contrast: creative work can be one person's task, but the activity itself does not require a shared artwork or journal.

## Documented procedure

1. Book a 25-, 50-, or 75-minute session; the platform pairs the user with another participant or a friend who books through an invite link.
2. At the start, partners greet each other and state what each plans to do. They may write a task in chat.
3. Both work quietly in the same video session. At the end, they report progress and close the session.

## Rules and gaps

- The help center encourages staying visible on camera and discourages recording or screenshotting a partner without consent.
- The sources do not require proof of completed work or that both partners work on the same kind of project.
- This is a synchronized session; it should not be collapsed into a daily asynchronous streak.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** time-slot booking, matching, shared start/end, reciprocal intention and report, co-presence without shared output. The software mediates matching and video access; the private work remains with each person.
