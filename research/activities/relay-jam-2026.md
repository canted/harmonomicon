# The Relay Jam 2026: staged game-making handoff

## Identity and evidence limit

This is a **published organizer procedure**, not evidence of a successful run. The [jam page](https://itch.io/jam/relay-jam-1) showed zero entries when consulted. Its value for the survey is the explicit two-phase rule and handoff requirement.

## Published procedure

1. Participants find a partner through the community forum, Discord, or their own network before the jam. The theme is revealed at the start.
2. Partner A uses the first half to create a prototype or foundation.
3. At the midpoint, A passes editable source/project files to B. The two may not work on the project simultaneously.
4. Partner B develops the project during the second half and submits a playable final game by the deadline.
5. The page describes a later exhibition/voting period for positive badges rather than an overall winner.

## Rules and gaps

- Groups of three or four may divide into an A group and B group, still with a sequential handoff. Solo participation is excluded.
- Partners need compatible tools and licensed assets. Clear communication about schedule, skill, and file organization is part of the published community rules.
- The organizer explicitly gives no guaranteed recovery if A disappears at handoff, except the possibility of finding another partner. The page does not define a verification method for the no-overlap rule or an automatic source-transfer channel.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** pre-activity matching, role phases, exclusive edit interval, artifact handoff, dependency between phases, missed-handoff recovery gap, later showcase. A digital host could schedule and record the handoff, but it cannot infer whether people worked simultaneously in external tools. This is a real published multi-stage jam structure; a hypothetical daily art/code phase sequence is not needed to establish the pattern.
