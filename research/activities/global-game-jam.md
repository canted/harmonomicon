# Global Game Jam

## Identity and context

- **Description:** People form teams and make games around a shared theme within a short event, with local sites and a global game listing.
- **Mode:** Usually a Friday-to-Sunday site event; sites may be physical or virtual. Global Game Jam (GGJ) explicitly frames its event as collaborative rather than a competition.
- **Facilitators:** Global organizers set the theme and upload rules; site organizers arrange the local schedule, team formation, help, and any showcase.

## Documented procedure

1. Register at a site. A site may hold a networking event before the jam. Participants receive the common theme on the edition's announced schedule; the traditional site kickoff includes a keynote and theme reveal.
2. Participants generate ideas, form teams, and make a game. A [documented 2024 site schedule](https://globalgamejam.org/group/75/announcements) puts brainstorming and team formation after theme reveal; this is a **site variant**, not a required global sequence.
3. Teams create their game project page during the jam. GGJ asks them to start it by about halfway through and to upload original work and source before the end.
4. Teams add files, instructions, video, and screenshots so others can discover and play the game. A site may close with a showcase.

## Rules, variants, and gaps

- The global theme is shared. [Diversifiers](https://globalgamejam.org/diversifiers) are optional secondary constraints that a team may choose; they are not points or a mandatory stage.
- [GGJ's theme page](https://globalgamejam.org/theme) says the 2026 edition planned a global reveal about a week before the jam, an exception to the traditional local-Friday reveal. Theme timing is therefore an edition rule, not an invariant of the activity.
- Site schedules vary. [GGJ's organizer guidance](https://globalgamejam.org/running-jam) describes pre-jam networking, kickoff/team formation, mentors, and a closing showcase as site options.
- A [2026 site announcement](https://globalgamejam.org/group/31088/announcements) used Discord for local voting and a physical display. That does not make voting a GGJ-wide rule.
- Sources do not prescribe an in-app progress feed, a fixed number of intermediate updates, team-internal chat, or a universal playtest checkpoint. The game-making work happens largely outside the GGJ site.

## Mechanisms and software boundary

**Mechanisms:** theme release, team formation, project membership, optional constraints, deadline, versioned project page, final upload, browsing/showcase. The site can mediate registration, team and project records, uploads, and discovery. Brainstorming, collaboration, playtesting, and promotion remain part of the human activity even when mediated by another tool or not recorded by the official site. A portable activity would have to state which of those interactions it actually coordinates.
