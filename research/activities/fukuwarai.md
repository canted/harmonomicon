# Fukuwarai

## Identity and context

- **Description:** A blindfolded player places cut-out facial features on a blank face while others give spoken guidance.
- **Context:** The Embassy of Japan in Kenya presents Fukuwarai as a Japanese children's game played around the New Year. This card describes that bulletin's version, not every practice of the game.
- **Group and mode:** Two or more, in person and live; no fixed group size is given.
- **Facilitator:** Someone arranges the materials and explains the rules. Other players guide the placer.

## Preparation

Place a paper face outline and separate facial-feature cutouts on a table. One participant uses a blindfold.

## Procedure

1. Spread the face outline on the table and give one player the feature pieces.
2. Blindfold that player.
3. The player places the pieces while others give spoken directions such as higher or left.
4. Once the pieces are placed, remove the blindfold and view the result.

## Rules and choices

- **Roles:** One placer with limited sight; the other players provide guidance.
- **Trigger:** Each placement is a participant action; the reveal follows placement of the pieces.
- **Visibility:** Guiders can see the board; the blindfolded placer cannot until the reveal.
- **Completion:** All pieces are placed and the blindfold is removed. The source gives no scoring or winner rule.

## Variants

None is specified in the cited bulletin.

## Exceptions and open questions

The source does not specify how guiders take turns, whether contradictory advice is allowed, whether a piece may be moved after placement, or whether every player gets a turn as placer.

## Access and participation

The documented version requires the placer to handle pieces and interpret spoken spatial cues; guiders need to perceive placements and communicate. The source does not offer adaptations. RNIB's [party guide](https://www.rnib.org.uk/documents/465/APDF-RE190403_Parties_and_Playdates-v07.pdf) stresses clear rules and precise spatial language for activities involving vision impairment, but it does not document a Fukuwarai variant. A participant's comfort with a blindfold must be checked rather than assumed.

## Mechanisms and possible software role

**Mechanisms:** Asymmetric information, many-to-one guidance, manual placement, final reveal. Software could provide a digital board and role-specific views, but that would be a new implementation. A physical host is sufficient for the cited game.
